# How to Write a Job Advert

### What makes you want to work for a company? Let's brainstorm

Create with your team a mock advert using the minimun requirements:

| <h4>Job Advert</h4><p>Company</p><ul><li>Design: name, industry, location, logo (include any other information you consider relevant)</li><li>Profile: a brief description of your company (4 or 5 lines) where you tell your applicants why they should want to work with you. Be creative! Consider what makes a company attractive to a candidate.</li></ul><p>Position</p><ul><li>Title of the position</li><li>Type of contract (full-time, part-time, internship)</li><li>Working conditions: working from an office, working from home, hybrid, requires travelling, etc.</li><li>Tasks and responsibilities: what your candidate will be doing at the position</li><li>Requirements: list the qualifications, work experience, skills and any other requirements for the position (own a vehicle, based in a certain location, special permits, etc.)</li><li>Compensation package: salary, bonuses, benefits.</li><li>How to apply</li></ul><p>Layout design</p><ul><li>Mock online job offer: your document should be a mock (an imitation) of the job offer your candidate clicked on to apply. You may use colours, pictures, clipart, canva, etc.</li></ul> |
