# How do we create a CV?

## CV Structure and Essential Sections

### 1. CV Heading

Include your basic professional information at the top of your CV:

* **Full name**
* **Professional title** (optional)
* **Career objective** (optional)

**Example:**

Jane Smith\
Graphic Designer\
Career Objective: To obtain a graphic design position within a creative agency where I can contribute my skills and creativity.

### 2. Contact Information

Provide the essential details that employers need to contact you:

* **Phone number**
* **Location:** Include only your city or town, rather than your full home address.
* **Email address**
* **LinkedIn profile** (optional)
* **Personal website or online portfolio** (optional)

Make sure all contact details are accurate and up to date.

### 3. Professional Summary or Career Objective

Write a brief statement highlighting your professional background, key strengths, career goals, and the value you could bring to an organisation.

Tailor this section to the specific position you are applying for, focusing on the skills and experience most relevant to the role.

### 4. Education

Present your educational background in reverse chronological order, starting with your most recent qualification.

For each entry, include:

* **Institution name**
* **Qualification or degree obtained**
* **Graduation date** or expected completion date
* **Relevant honours, awards, or academic achievements**, where applicable.

### 5. Work Experience

List your professional experience in reverse chronological order, beginning with your most recent position.

For each role, provide:

* **Job title**
* **Company name and location**
* **Employment dates:** Include the month and year of starting and finishing.
* **Key responsibilities and achievements:** Use bullet points to describe your main duties, contributions, and measurable accomplishments.

Focus on relevant experience and highlight results whenever possible.

### 6. Skills

Highlight the abilities and competencies that are relevant to the position you are applying for.

Organise your skills into clear categories, such as:

* **Technical Skills:** Software, programming languages, operating systems, tools, and other technical knowledge.
* **Language Skills:** Languages you speak and your proficiency level in each one.
* **Soft Skills:** Communication, teamwork, problem-solving, adaptability, organisation, and time management.

Be specific and honest when describing your proficiency level.
