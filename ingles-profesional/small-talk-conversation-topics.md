# Small talk conversation topics

## Small Talk (Persiflage)

> _"It’s not that small."_

***

### 1. What is Small Talk?

**Discussion / Warm-up:**

* Reflect on what "small talk" means to you.
* Discuss your definition and initial thoughts with your teacher or group.

#### Definition: Persiflage

**Persiflage** is a light, flippant, and/or mocking manner of speech or writing.

* **Synonyms / Related terms:** _Banter_, _idle chatter_, _small talk_.

***

### 2. When to Use Small Talk

**Discussion:**

* In which situations or contexts would you naturally use small talk? (e.g., networking events, elevator rides, waiting before a meeting, social gatherings).

| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Safe / Appropriate**              | Weather, Movies, Sports, Cars, Hometown, Holidays, Gardening, Favorite celebrities                                                                             | Neutral, lighthearted, and easy for anyone to engage with without conflict.                   |

### 4. Rules & Structure of Small Talk

**Discussion Question:**

* Are there unspoken rules or structural guidelines when engaging in small talk?

#### Key Guidelines for Successful Small Talk

1. **Keep it light:** Focus on low-stakes, positive, or neutral subjects.
2. **Active Listening & Open Questions:** Ask follow-up questions based on what the other person shares.
3. **Balance the Airtime:** Maintain an even exchange of speaking and listening; avoid dominating the conversation.
4. **Read the Room:** Pay attention to body language and know when to gracefully transition or wrap up the conversation.
