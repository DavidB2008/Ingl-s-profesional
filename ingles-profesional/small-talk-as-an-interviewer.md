# Small Talk as an Interviewer

## Interview Icebreakers & Small Talk Guide

A collection of introductory phrases, light conversation starters, and strategies to put candidates at ease during interviews while maintaining professional boundaries.

***

### 1. Icebreaker Examples by Category

#### General Icebreakers

* _"How’s your day going so far?"_
* _"Did you have any trouble finding our office / joining the call?"_
* _"I hope the weather isn’t too bad where you are!"_
* _"Did you do anything fun this past weekend?"_

#### Industry-Related Light Chat

* _"I saw on your resume that you've worked in \[industry/field] for a while. What got you interested?"_
* _"Our team recently \[mention something interesting]. Have you worked on anything similar?"_
* _"It's an exciting time in \[industry]. Have you been following any trends?"_

#### Casual but Professional

* _"I noticed on your LinkedIn that you're based in \[city]. How do you like it there?"_
* _"I always like to start with a bit of small talk. Are you a coffee or tea person?"_
* _"I see that you enjoy \[hobby/interest]. How did you get into that?"_

***

### 2. Handling Candidate Nerves

Use these comforting phrases if the candidate appears anxious or tense:

* _"Let me know if you need to take a break at any time and feel free to ask me anything."_
* _"Interviews can be nerve-wracking, but this is just a conversation."_
* _"I know interviews can feel formal, but feel free to take a breath and relax; it's just a chat!"_

***

### 3. Tips for Effective Small Talk

To ensure icebreakers serve their purpose effectively, keep the following principles in mind:

* **Keep it light and positive:** Avoid overly complex, sensitive, or heavy topics at the start.
* **Show genuine interest:** Listen actively to their responses rather than treating it like a checklist.
* **Adapt dynamically:** Read their body language and tone to match their personality and comfort level.
* **Transition smoothly:** Briefly acknowledge their answers before shifting naturally into the formal interview questions.
